The method

Three ways to work with your breath.

There is no single breath for every state. Learn when to settle, how to build better breathing habits, and when to explore something deeper.

01 · Change your state

Relax

Simple breathing tools for when you feel wired, overwhelmed or unable to switch off.

02 · Change your habits

Retrain

Understand how you breathe every day and practise small changes at rest and during exercise.

03 · Connect with your body

Release

Explore deeper guided experiences with breath, music and space to go at your own pace.

Meet Suraj

My mind understood. My body didn't.

Therapy, books and meditation helped me understand my anxiety. Breathwork gave me a different way to connect with what was happening in my body.

The Breath Reset grew out of that experience, and a wish to help others discover what their breath can do for them.
