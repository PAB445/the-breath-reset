The idea

There is no single breath for every state.

Most breathing advice comes down to "slow down and take a deep breath". Sometimes that helps. A lot of the time it's the wrong tool. A wired body, a scattered mind and a hard training session all need something different.

The method

Three ways to work with your breath.

01

Relax

Change your state.

Simple tools for when you feel wired, overwhelmed, restless or can't switch off. Slower rhythms and longer exhales you can use on the train, at your desk or lying in bed.

Coherent breathing · Longer exhales · Slow breathing · Sleep

02

Retrain

Change the way you breathe.

Most of us rarely think about how we breathe throughout the day. Retrain helps you understand your breathing habits and make small changes that can improve how you breathe at rest, during exercise and in everyday life.

Nasal breathing · Breathing mechanics · CO₂ tolerance · Exercise

03

Release

Connect more deeply with your body.

These longer, immersive sessions use breath, music and movement to help you move out of your head and into your body. There’s space to explore sensations and emotions, with the freedom to go at your own pace.

Connected breathing · Music · Movement · Integration

Each has its place.

Relax when you need to settle. Retrain how you breathe every day. Release when you’re ready to explore deeper.

The goal isn’t to feel calm all the time. It’s to become more comfortable with different states and learn how to work with your breath through them.

The programme

Six weeks. A different relationship with your breath.

Learn how your breath influences the way you feel, and how to use it in everyday life.

Over six weeks, we’ll explore how to recognise your state, use different breathing techniques to shift how you feel, and build greater comfort with the sensations and emotions that arise in your body.

Each session combines interesting theory, practical experiments and guided breathwork. You’ll leave with something new to practise each week, building a toolkit you can keep using long after the programme ends.

  1. Week 01 — THE POWER OF BREATH

    Notice

    Discover how your breathing changes with your state, and how changing your breath can influence how you feel.

    We’ll experiment with different breathing patterns so you can experience these changes for yourself.

  2. Week 02 — RETRAIN

    Breathe Better

    Explore how you breathe throughout the day, from nasal breathing and breathing mechanics to the role of CO₂.

    Learn simple ways to improve your everyday breathing habits.

  3. Week 03 — RELAX

    Find Your Calm

    Learn how to use slower breathing, steady rhythms and longer exhales to help you settle when you’re feeling stressed, overwhelmed or unable to switch off.

    Take away practical techniques you can use at work, at home or before bed.

  4. Week 04 — BUILD CAPACITY

    Explore Your Range

    Being regulated doesn’t mean being calm all the time.

    Explore how different breathing patterns can create activation or help you settle, and practise staying present with different sensations.

    Learn when to use different approaches and how to work with your breath during moments of stress or physical activity.

  5. Week 05 — RELEASE

    Go Deeper

    Move beyond everyday breathing techniques into a longer, immersive breathwork experience.

    Using fuller breathing, music and guided practice, we’ll create space to connect more deeply with your body and explore whatever sensations or emotions arise.

  6. Week 06 — RELEASE & INTEGRATE

    Let Go

    Our final session explores conscious connected breathing, with less instruction and more space to follow your own experience.

    We’ll finish by reflecting on what you’ve learnt and bringing together a personal breathing toolkit you can continue using.

Every week includes

  1. Understand
    An interesting concept about breathing and how it affects you.
  2. Experiment
    Try different breathing techniques and notice what happens.
  3. Experience
    A longer guided breathwork session to put it into practice.
  4. Take home
    A simple breathing exercise to practise during the week.
  5. Reflect
    Notice what’s changing and how you can apply what you’ve learnt.

It’s not about getting good at breathwork.

It’s about what changes outside the sessions. Feeling more equipped when you’re stressed, understanding your breathing habits, being more present in your body, and having practices you know how to use when you need them.

Why I do this

My mind understood. My body didn't.

I've had anxiety since my late teens. From the outside I was fine. I went to work, saw friends, went to the gym. Inside, my body felt like it was bracing for something, even when nothing was wrong.

Therapy, books and meditation all helped. I got better at noticing my thought loops and not getting pulled into them, and honestly, that changed my life. But my body was still on edge.

"What am I thinking?" "What's happening in my body?"

Three years ago I did my first breathwork session. I remember leaving with a sense of relief. I already understood my anxiety. This was the first time my body seemed to soften with it. The Breath Reset grew out of that.

Now I want to help other people discover what their breath can do for them, whether that’s finding a little more calm in everyday life or exploring something deeper.

How sessions work

You're in charge of your breath.

Every experience is different, and you’re always in control of how far you take it. There’s no pressure to push yourself or have a particular experience.

You can always stop

You’re welcome to slow down, return to your natural breathing, open your eyes or stop completely at any point. You never need to explain why.

We check before going deeper

More intense breathing isn’t suitable for everyone. Before a deeper session, we’ll ask about your health and any relevant medical conditions to make sure the practice is appropriate for you. Some people may need to avoid certain techniques or seek medical clearance first.

Intensity isn’t the goal

Some people feel calm. Others experience strong sensations or emotions. There’s no right way to feel, and you don’t need to have a breakthrough for the experience to be worthwhile.

Breathwork isn’t therapy

Breathwork can be a useful complement to other forms of care, but it doesn’t replace medical or mental health treatment.
