Past experiences

The Inner Exhale

A three-hour breathwork experience at Mothership Studios in Marrickville, co-hosted with Bianca.

When
Sunday 11 October 2026
Where
Mothership Studios, Marrickville
Length
3 hours
Breathwork
75-minute guided flow

We started with how breathing actually works, then ran short experiments so everyone could feel the difference between patterns. From there the breathing got fuller and the music got louder, building into a long guided flow. We finished with stillness, conversation and a few simple tools to take home.
