Your six weeks

From noticing
to knowing.

Start by understanding your everyday breath. Build ways to settle and explore your range, before moving into longer, more immersive experiences.

No previous breathwork experience is needed. Each week builds on the last, with space to go at your own pace.

  1. Week 01 · The power of breath

    Notice your breath

    Discover how your breathing reflects how you feel, and explore how different rhythms can change your experience. Learn to recognise when you feel settled, wired or flat.

    Begin with a five-minute coherent breathing practice: a gentle, steady rhythm to explore each day.

  2. Week 02 · Retrain

    Breathe with more ease

    Get to know your everyday breathing through simple observations and experiments. Explore nasal breathing, breathing mechanics and the role of carbon dioxide.

    Practise Breathe Light: gentle, quiet, comfortable breathing for five minutes a day.

  3. Week 03 · Relax

    Find your downshift

    Explore slower breathing, longer exhales and humming. Notice your early signs of stress and try different ways of responding, without needing to force yourself to feel calm.

    Create a personal downshift plan: one familiar situation, an early warning sign and a practice to try.

  4. Week 04 · Build capacity

    Explore your range

    Being regulated doesn’t mean being calm all the time. Explore both activating and settling practices, and notice how it feels to move between different states.

    Learn to choose an approach for the moment, with room to adjust or return to natural breathing.

  5. Week 05 · Release

    Go deeper

    Bring the awareness you’ve built into a longer guided breath journey. Fuller breathing, music and guidance offer space to explore sensations and emotions at your own pace.

    Practise staying curious about your experience, without chasing a breakthrough or a particular feeling.

  6. Week 06 · Release & integrate

    Make it your own

    Explore conscious connected breathing with less instruction and more space to follow your experience. We close by reflecting on what you’ve noticed across the six weeks.

    Bring your practices together into a personal breathing toolkit you can keep using beyond the programme.

The weekly rhythm

Time to learn.
Space to experience.

We begin with a check-in, then explore the week’s theme through practical teaching and breathing experiments. A guided breath journey gives you time to put it into practice, followed by a moment to reflect.

You’ll leave with printed notes and a simple practice to explore between sessions. We return to what you noticed the following week, so your toolkit grows from your own experience.

At your own pace

You're in charge of your breath.

Every experience is different, and you’re always in control of how far you take it. There’s no pressure to push yourself or have a particular experience.

You can always stop

You’re welcome to slow down, return to your natural breathing, open your eyes or stop completely at any point. You never need to explain why.

We check before going deeper

More intense breathing isn’t suitable for everyone. Before a deeper session, we’ll ask about your health and any relevant medical conditions to make sure the practice is appropriate for you. Some people may need to avoid certain techniques or seek medical clearance first.

Intensity isn’t the goal

Some people feel calm. Others experience strong sensations or emotions. There’s no right way to feel, and you don’t need to have a breakthrough for the experience to be worthwhile.

Breathwork isn’t therapy

Breathwork can be a useful complement to other forms of care, but it doesn’t replace medical or mental health treatment.
