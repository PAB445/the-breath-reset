Past experiences

The Inner Exhale

11 October 2026 · Marrickville, Sydney

Three hours to slow down, explore the breath and connect.

At Mothership Studios, Bianca and I brought a small group together for practical experiments and a 75-minute guided breathwork flow.

We started with how breathing works, then explored different patterns together. As the breath got fuller and the music louder, we moved into a deeper guided experience.

We finished with stillness, conversation and a few simple tools to take home.

“This workshop gave me so much peace through presence. It’s emotional, powerful, and uplifting. If you’re reading this, look for me at the next one.”

Bakari, Inner Exhale participant
